METHODOLOGY & RECONCILIATION GOVERNANCE NOTES

• Source Data: Historical daily closing prices and trading volumes retrieved from Yahoo Finance for AAPL, AMZN, MSFT, NVDA, and TSLA.
• Annualized Return (CAGR): Computed using 252 trading days per year convention: (P_end / P_start)^(252 / (N - 1)) - 1.
• Risk Metrics: Annualized volatility = std(daily_return) * sqrt(252); Sharpe Ratio uses a 4.0% annualized risk-free benchmark; Maximum Drawdown reflects peak-to-trough decline strictly within the active filter slice.
• Disclaimer: Analytical portfolio intelligence for historical risk/return evaluation only; historical performance does not guarantee future returns.
