# Executive Insights & Strategic Recommendations — Executive Equity Performance & Risk Analytics

**Prepared by:** Abraham Adetola, PMP®, PMI-PBA®, CSM®, CSPO®

1. **Risk-Adjusted Equity Evaluation:** Evaluate total returns alongside annualized volatility, maximum drawdown, and Sharpe/Sortino ratios rather than relying on cumulative price growth alone.
2. **Concentration & Drawdown Controls:** Monitor peak-to-trough drawdown exposure across high-beta holdings (such as `TSLA` and `NVDA`) relative to lower-volatility compounders.
3. **KPI Governance:** Maintain unified 252-day annualization conventions, aligned trading calendars, and automated unit-test reconciliation across reporting layers.

*(Historical market analytics only; does not constitute investment advice or guarantee future performance.)*
